LASER · 01 / 03

Selective correction with
cross-layer evidence

Layer disagreement alone does not establish that a revision is reliable.

01 · Need

Should we revise?

Read layer distributions through the shared LM head. Estimate correction need with JSD.

02 · Support

Is evidence reliable?

Assess cross-layer support and directional advantage in a shared candidate space.

03 · Gate

Selective fusion

Fuse only when both conditions hold. Otherwise preserve the original distribution.
